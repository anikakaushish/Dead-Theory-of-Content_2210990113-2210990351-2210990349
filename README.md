## Description
Dead Theory of Content states that the web will be filled with computer-generated content and less authentic human activity. The content shared on the internet, such as social media messages, news, etc., is either machine-made or is influenced heavily by them. Hence, there arise some issues about the fate of the Internet and its future. Will it remain a platform of authentic human creativity or will become a playground for computers?
